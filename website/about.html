About

Matt Mohn

I'm Matt. I built and maintain Mosaic.

I've had a lifelong interest in how redistricting shapes, skews, and plagues the political process here in the United States. I built Mosaic because I believe people working to challenge gerrymandering deserve tools as powerful as those used to create it.

I graduated from Duke, where I studied public policy and statistics. I grew up in Texas and now work as a pollster in Washington, D.C.
